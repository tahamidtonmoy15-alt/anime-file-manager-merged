How to use: Set each anime to Watched, Unwatched or Watching. JSON backups include anime details, statuses, Watching progress and poster references. Episode video files remain in app storage; use the File Manager download button to export a video file. Use the Watch Lists and Categories filters to organize your collection. Open Episodes on an anime card to manage local video files.
Data: Anime details and watch status are stored on this device. Use Export and Import to back up your library. Episode videos are stored in the app's browser storage; storage limits depend on the device and available space.
Posters: Posters are loaded from public anime artwork sources when available. You can also add your own poster when creating an anime.
